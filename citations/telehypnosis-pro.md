## Cross-repository reference: Imran/InCube dream-state sensory-delivery patent family

**Added 2026-07-08, per operator approval.** Cross-reference to a compartment in the operator's private research repository, which holds the full digest, verbatim claims, and primary-source PDFs. The repository and compartment path are deliberately not recorded here.

**What it is:** US Patent family (WO2014152887A1; granted US9,620,027 B2, US10,019,908 B2, US10,242,585 B2, US10,685,577 B2 — all four now expired for nonpayment of maintenance fees), inventors Mir Imran & Joel M. Harris, assignee Incube Laboratories LLC. A wearable EEG/EOG headband detects REM sleep and a "period of optimal receptivity to learning" (PORL) — defined in the claims as a window where the EEG signal is stable in the 7.5–11.5 Hz (alpha) band at 30–50 µV — and synchronizes audio delivery to that window, for stated purposes including "delivering messages to the user's subconscious."

**Why it's relevant here, and what it is NOT:**
- **Relevant as a detection-technique reference**, not a validated-efficacy reference. The patent's REM/alpha-band EEG detection method is legitimate, established sleep-neuroscience signal processing (traceable to a verified real Hobson, Spagna & Malenka 1978 *Science* paper) — if Telehypnosis Pro or the Mindforge entrainment tool ever add real-time receiver-state detection (as opposed to the current fixed-duration Gateway/Sleep Onset presets), this patent's alpha-band stability-window approach is a concrete, citable prior-art method worth reviewing.
- **Not relevant, and should not be cited, as support for "audio delivered during a detected brain state produces subconscious learning/messaging."** The patent supplies no experimental data for that claim anywhere in its own text — that repository's digest confirms that explicitly. Do not use this patent as a citation for Telehypnosis Pro's or Mindforge's own claimed effects; it is architecturally/thematically adjacent (both concern timing sensory input to a detected/altered brain state), not evidentially supporting.
- The two real, peer-reviewed research programs that repository's digest identifies as the correct comparison points — Targeted Memory Reactivation (Rudoy et al., *Science* 2009, slow-wave sleep) and Targeted Dream Incubation/Dormio (Horowitz et al., *Consciousness and Cognition* 2020, hypnagogic stage) — are closer to genuine evidentiary relatives of this suite's own claims than the Imran patent is, and are worth their own citation-auditor review if Telehypnosis Pro or Mindforge ever cite sleep-stage-timed sensory delivery as a mechanism.
