# Coherence — Citation Record

Bibliographic records for `coherence.html`'s research basis, per the Prior-Art Protocol in `CLAUDE.md`. Every entry below was verified during the citation audit of 2026-08-05 / 2026-08-08 against Crossref, PubMed, or the publisher of record. Nothing here is cited from memory.

**Verification key:** ✅ verified against Crossref/PubMed · 📄 verified against publisher or archive copy · ⚠️ exists but the finding is disputed or failed replication · ❌ claim not supported by any source found

## Heart-rate-variability coherence

- 📄 **McCraty, R., Atkinson, M., Tomasino, D., & Bradley, R.T. (2009).** The coherent heart: heart–brain interactions, psychophysiological coherence, and the emergence of system-wide order. *Integral Review*, 5(2), 10–115.
  No Crossref DOI. Publisher copy: https://www.integral-review.org/issues/vol_5_no_2_mccraty_et_al_the_coherent_heart.pdf
  Source of the ~0.1 Hz resonance target (the ~5–6 breaths/minute pacing the tool implements) and of the "coherence" construct itself.

  ⚠️ **Two things to keep in view when citing this.** *Integral Review* is an open peer-reviewed journal but is not indexed in MEDLINE or the Web of Science core collection, and the paper is authored entirely by HeartMath Institute staff describing HeartMath's own construct — it is a monograph-length position paper, not an independent replication. The **physiological** claim it rests on (that paced breathing near 0.1 Hz produces a large, reproducible increase in HRV amplitude via baroreflex resonance) is uncontroversial and well replicated in the mainstream HRV-biofeedback literature. The broader claims layered on top of it are not. Cite it for the resonance frequency; do not cite it for the wider framework without independent support.

## Distant-intention material

  No Crossref DOI. Journal archive: https://journals.sfu.ca/seemj/index.php/seemj/article/view/112
  The IONS DMILS series. Relevant to this tool only insofar as the coherence primer is shared with the two-person protocols — **coherence training itself makes no psi claim and needs no psi citation.**

  ⚠️ The effect size `d=0.11` attributed to this series elsewhere in the suite (see `citations/telehypnosis-pro.md`) has **not** been traced to a specific table in the paper. Until it is, do not repeat the number.

## Not claimed

This tool teaches paced breathing and logs sessions. It does not measure your heart rate — there is no sensor input of any kind. Nothing in it can tell you whether you achieved coherence; the star rating is self-report. Any statement implying the tool *detects* a physiological state would be false.
